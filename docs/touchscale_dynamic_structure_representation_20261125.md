# TouchScale dynamic tactile spatial-structure representation

Date: 2026-11-25
Branch: thesis-few-shot-structure-20261125

## Purpose
Build and audit a time-varying, interpretable tactile representation before training any predictor or meta-learner. This stage does not claim a validated anatomical graph and does not fit a model.

## Data
Reuse the deterministic 300-Episode sample generated by `audit_touchscale_signal_300.choose_sample` from the existing 15 configured shards. The sample remains scene-proportional. Preserve actor, scene, task, date, shard, and Episode identifiers for later grouped evaluation.

## Per-frame pad load
For each hand and each of the 15 native pads, sum the nonnegative normal-force values across taxels to obtain one pad-load value in N per frame. The 15 channels are retained in their native dataset order. No geometric adjacency, pad centroid, or anatomical distance is assumed.

## Temporal representation
1. Use the hand group's `t_epoch` timestamps. Reject missing, non-finite, non-monotonic, or length-mismatched timestamps; do not silently substitute frame indices.
2. Divide the complete timestamp interval into 10 equal-duration bins.
3. In each bin, calculate the mean and 95th percentile load for each pad (15 + 15 channels).
4. Define active frames using the dataset's aggregate contact convention: total load over all pads > 3 N.
5. On active frames only, calculate each pad's normalized share of total load and average those per-frame shares within the bin (15 channels).
6. From the 15-dimensional share vector, calculate normalized entropy, HHI concentration, top-1 share, top-3 share, and dominant pad index.
7. Between adjacent bins with active contact in both bins, calculate pad-share L1 change, Jensen-Shannon divergence (bits), and cosine similarity.

## Output
- `time_bin_dynamic_features.csv`: one row per Episode × hand × temporal bin; includes all 15 pad loads, 15 pad shares, and interpretable dynamics.
- `episode_hand_dynamic_summary.csv`: per-hand quality, coverage, and aggregate transition summaries.
- `sample_selection.csv`, `detail.json`, and per-shard SHA-256 hashes.

## Quality and limitations
- Bins with fewer than five frames are rejected rather than silently merged.
- A bin without active frames retains raw pad-load features but its normalized active-contact distribution is missing.
- The representation describes pad-level loading dynamics; it is not a validated spatial adjacency graph.
- This is a representation and QC stage only. It is not a prediction experiment, cross-object transfer evaluation, or few-shot adaptation experiment.
- Any later split must group by independent Episode and explicitly control actor/task/object leakage.

## Next decision gate
Inspect coverage, missingness, feature distributions, and actor/scene heterogeneity. Only then define a target and grouped train/validation/test protocol for a simple baseline. Reptile remains deferred until a meaningful task distribution and a fair ordinary fine-tuning baseline are established.
